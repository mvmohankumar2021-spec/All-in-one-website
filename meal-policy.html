Meal service policies and declarations

Version: 26 September 2026. These operational declarations supplement the Partner Agreement and do not replace applicable law or statutory customer rights.

Food Safety & Hygiene Declaration

I confirm that the kitchen and relevant registration information supplied is accurate and current. I will maintain appropriate hygiene, safe water, food storage, temperature control, packaging and transport practices, disclose known allergens and cross-contact limitations, and provide clear storage and consumption guidance. I will not promise accommodation of dietary needs that the kitchen cannot safely meet.

Ordering, delivery and complaint policy

I will publish accurate menus, portions, prices, charges, order cutoffs, delivery coverage and time estimates before customers order. I will disclose cancellation and refund terms and provide a clear contact process for missing, incorrect, spilled, damaged or unsafe meals. Complaints will be reviewed fairly using reasonable evidence, with appropriate replacement or refund remedies; these terms do not exclude statutory rights.

Subscriptions and reusable containers

Where offered, I will disclose plan duration, meals included, delivery days, pause/skip notice, credits, renewal consent, cancellation and unused-meal refund terms before purchase. Any reusable-container deposit, return deadline, cleaning responsibility and refund conditions will be stated in advance. I will not introduce undisclosed charges or automatic renewal without appropriate consent.
