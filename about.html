OUR STORY

Inspired by the wish-fulfilling tree.

The name SHAKALPA is inspired by Kalpataru, or Karpaga Virutcham — the wish-fulfilling tree. It represents abundance and the fulfilment of wishes and needs.

SHAKALPA is envisioned as a digital destination where people can find solutions for a wide variety of everyday, professional, and business requirements. From a simple local service to a major business requirement, our aim is to bring possibilities together in one place.

OUR VISION

A trusted, accessible destination.

To help people search, find, and fulfil their needs while enabling businesses of every size to become more visible and reach the right customers.

OUR MISSION

Close the gap between need and fulfilment.

  • Make finding businesses, products, and services simple.
  • Bring diverse categories together on one platform.
  • Help customers make informed choices with accurate information, ratings, reviews, and appropriate verification.
  • Strengthen the digital presence of local businesses, professionals, startups, SMEs, manufacturers, and established companies.

FOR CUSTOMERS

Search what you need. Find the right options. Fulfil your requirement.

SHAKALPA brings everyday household needs, healthcare, education, shopping, travel, professional services, technology, real estate, events, and business services together.

FOR BUSINESSES

A digital space to be found when it matters.

Whether you are an individual professional, local shop, service provider, startup, SME, manufacturer, distributor, or established business, SHAKALPA helps customers find you when they need you.
