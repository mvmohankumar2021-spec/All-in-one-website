Household supply partner declarations

Version: 26 September 2026. These operational declarations supplement the Partner Agreement, do not replace applicable law and do not exclude statutory customer rights.

Product Authenticity, Quality & Safety Declaration

I will supply genuine products with accurate labels, directions, warnings and support for special claims. I will maintain relevant supplier and manufacturer/importer records, check batches/expiry where applicable, store incompatible products separately and use suitable packaging and transport precautions. Relevant safety data sheets will be available for applicable chemical products. Unsafe, expired, damaged, leaking or recalled goods will be withheld from sale and handled appropriately.

Delivery, cancellation, returns and refunds

I will disclose prices, quantities, fees, order limits and fulfilment times before purchase. Substitutions or price changes require customer approval. I will publish cancellation, failed-delivery, return and refund terms, including opened/sealed personal-care product conditions, without excluding customer rights. Wrong, missing, expired, damaged or leaking products and safety concerns will receive a clear reporting process, fair review and appropriate remedies. Manufacturer warranties will be represented accurately only for eligible goods.

Drinking Water Quality & Hygiene Declaration

For drinking-water supply, I will accurately disclose my role, sources, brands and applicable permissions. I will maintain relevant quality/testing or supplier evidence, safe storage, container/tanker hygiene and transport controls according to the activities performed. Deposits, container returns/cleaning, refund conditions and recurring delivery prices, pause/skip/cancellation and renewal consent will be disclosed before agreement. I will investigate contamination concerns promptly and withhold affected supplies where appropriate.
