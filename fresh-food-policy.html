Fresh food partner declarations

Version: 26 September 2026. These operational declarations supplement the Partner Agreement; they do not replace applicable law or exclude statutory customer rights.

Food Safety, Hygiene & Quality Declaration

I will provide accurate registration, sourcing and product information, maintain relevant purchase records, inspect freshness and expiry, and use suitable storage and transport temperature controls. I will maintain cleaning, pest control, handler hygiene, safe water/ice where used, appropriate raw-food separation and relevant allergen information. Unsafe, spoiled, expired or recalled stock will be withheld from sale and handled appropriately. Certification and origin claims will be supported by relevant evidence.

Delivery, processing, cancellation and refunds

I will disclose quantities, prices, fees, delivery coverage and timing before purchase. Substitutions and price changes require customer approval. For cleaning/cutting, I will disclose whether charging is based on weight before or after processing, the processing charges and how final-weight differences affect the price.

I will publish reasonable cancellation, perishable-return and refund terms, plus a clear reporting and fair review process for spoiled, damaged, incorrect, missing or short-weight goods. Appropriate replacement/refund remedies will be offered without excluding applicable customer rights.

Where recurring orders are offered, plan prices, delivery schedules, renewal consent, pause/skip rules, cancellation and refund conditions will be disclosed before agreement. No undisclosed charges or automatic renewal without appropriate consent.
