Grocery partner declarations

Version: 26 September 2026. These operational declarations supplement the Partner Agreement. They do not replace applicable law or limit statutory customer rights.

Food Safety & Product Quality Declaration

I will provide accurate registration and product information, source goods responsibly, retain relevant supplier records, check expiry dates and maintain appropriate storage, cleaning, pest control and temperature controls. I will handle loose foods hygienically, use suitable packaging, provide relevant allergen information and measure quantities accurately. Expired, unsafe, damaged or recalled products will be withheld from sale and handled appropriately.

I will support organic and other certification claims with relevant evidence and will not make misleading claims about certification, origin, grade or quality.

Delivery, cancellation, returns and refunds

I will disclose prices, applicable MRP, quantities, packaging/tax/delivery charges, minimum orders, fulfilment estimates and cancellation terms before purchase. Unavailable products will not be substituted without customer consent.

I will publish pickup and failed-delivery arrangements, reasonable perishable-return conditions and refund timelines. Missing, incorrect, expired, damaged or short-supplied goods will have a clear reporting and fair review process with appropriate replacement or refund remedies. Bulk shortages and transport damage will be reviewed using reasonable evidence, not automatic rejection or undisclosed deductions.

Any optional credit terms will be agreed separately before supply. Profile payment details are reused and must not be entered in public product descriptions.
