Retail partner declarations

Version: 26 September 2026. These operational declarations supplement the Partner Agreement. They do not replace applicable law or exclude statutory customer rights.

Product Authenticity, Quality & Safety Declaration

I will provide genuine goods with accurate descriptions, sourcing records, relevant instructions and safety warnings. Toy age suitability, small-parts warnings, battery requirements and relevant compliance information will be disclosed. Book editions and used-book condition, including known missing/damaged pages, will be represented accurately. Unsafe, damaged or recalled goods will be withheld from sale and handled appropriately. Manufacturer warranties will be represented accurately only for eligible products.

Orders, delivery, cancellation, returns and refunds

I will disclose prices, quantities, charges, order limits and fulfilment estimates before purchase. Substitutions and price changes require customer approval. I will publish cancellation, return, exchange and refund terms and fairly review missing, incorrect, damaged or short-supplied goods using reasonable evidence and appropriate remedies.

Custom/personalised and special-order work will have clear specifications, customer approval, lead times and cancellation/refund terms. Customer files collected for printing or personalisation will be used only for authorised purposes, protected against unnecessary access and retained/deleted according to disclosed terms.

Shopping-centre Operator Declaration

I confirm my authority to represent the centre and will provide accurate management, directory, facilities, accessibility, parking, security/help-desk and emergency information. I will maintain relevant operator permissions and applicable building, occupancy and fire-safety evidence. Individual tenant listings and operator responsibilities will be distinguished; a centre listing does not imply that every tenant is approved or that the operator sells every listed product.
