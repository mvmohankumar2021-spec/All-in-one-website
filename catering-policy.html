Catering safety and service declaration

Platform onboarding declaration for Wedding, Party, Corporate, Home and Outdoor Catering. This does not replace applicable law or your event-specific written quotation.

Food safety & hygiene declaration

I confirm that my registration/licence information is accurate and that I will operate within its applicable scope. I will maintain hygienic preparation, trained staff, safe water, suitable storage, safe transport and appropriate temperature controls.

I will disclose known ingredients and allergens, explain cross-contact limitations, and accept special dietary requests only when they can be safely supported. I will maintain appropriate cleaning, pest prevention, waste and leftover-food procedures.

For on-site and outdoor work, I will agree venue access, power, water, equipment and weather contingency arrangements before the event. I will promptly communicate any incident affecting food safety or delivery.

Booking, quality and damage claims

I will give customers a clear written quotation covering the menu, guest count, serving arrangements, inclusions, taxes, additional charges, payment schedule and guest-count deadline before confirmation.

I will disclose cancellation, rescheduling and refund terms before booking. I will provide a complaint contact, fairly review evidence of service-quality concerns, and communicate the proposed resolution and timeline.

Equipment or property damage claims must identify the incident, supporting evidence and claimed amount. Responsibility and any compensation will be reviewed fairly with the affected parties; no automatic admission of liability or deduction is created by this declaration.

Food quality concerns are handled through complaint resolution, not a repair-style warranty. Applicable consumer rights remain unaffected.
